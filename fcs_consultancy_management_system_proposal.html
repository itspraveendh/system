System Vision

A Clear Plan for Your Business Management System

The objective is to plan and guide the implementation of a centralized management system that helps FCS Consultancy organize its business operations, employee records, financial activities, and administrative workflows.

Note: The modules below represent planned system capabilities to be guided during development.

1. Employee Management

Organize employee information, attendance, and leave records.

2. Financial Management

Plan salary, payroll, invoice, income, expenses, and revenue tracking.

3. Centralized Administration

Structure the admin panel, user roles, permissions, and management workflows.

4. Reporting & Insights

Plan revenue summaries, expense reports, outstanding balances, and business statistics.

Scope of Consulting

What My Professional Service Includes

End-to-end project planning and guided implementation support, with coding handled by the client/developer.

01

Requirement Analysis & Planning

Understand business requirements, identify modules, define project scope, and prepare the development roadmap.

02

System Architecture

Plan the PHP project structure, folder organization, reusable components, file responsibilities, and development workflow.

03

UI/UX & Dashboard Planning

Define the dashboard layout, navigation, tables, forms, charts, revenue summaries, and business statistics.

04

AI-Assisted Development Guidance

Prepare effective ChatGPT prompts and guide the use of Google Gemini for code generation, refinement, and troubleshooting.

05

Database Design & Setup

Plan MySQL tables, fields, relationships, and database structure. Guide XAMPP and local development environment setup.

06

Management Module Planning

Guide implementation planning for employee management, salary, payroll, invoices, clients, projects, attendance, expenses, and revenue.

07

Admin Panel & Access Control

Plan login, authentication, user roles, permissions, and CRUD operations for managing records.

08

Integration, Testing & Debugging

Assist with connecting modules, identifying errors, troubleshooting PHP/MySQL issues, and conducting functional tests.

09

Hosting & Deployment Guidance

Explain hosting requirements, domain configuration, live database migration, environment settings, and deployment checks.

10

Documentation & Final Handover

Prepare implementation documentation, explain workflows, and guide the final project walkthrough.

Project Agreement

Scope of Service & Project Conditions

Clear boundaries and understandings ensure smooth collaboration and clarity throughout the engagement.

The service covers project planning, system architecture, AI-assisted development guidance, and guided implementation support.
The client/developer is responsible for writing, implementing, and maintaining the code.
Full independent software development and delivery of a complete production-ready application are not included in this professional fee.
Unlimited revisions and additional features outside the agreed scope are excluded unless separately agreed.
Domain registration, hosting, paid AI subscriptions, third-party services, and other external costs are not included.
Testing and debugging support will be provided within the agreed scope.
Security, data protection, deployment, and production-readiness responsibilities must be clearly agreed upon before launch.
The project schedule and specific deliverables will be agreed upon before work begins.
